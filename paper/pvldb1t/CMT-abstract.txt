We measure routed inverted-file search over one trillion 4-bit quantized vectors against the exact scan of the same compressed index. The index is 500 servers of two billion rows, 24 TB, built from a seeded synthetic corpus on the National Research Platform's shared Kubernetes cluster. Probing 32 of 2048 cells, routed search returns 0.989 of the exact top ten for queries that are corpus rows and 0.969 for queries the index does not contain, and probing 128 cells it returns 0.999 for both. Two hundred further queries, one from each of 200 shards, measured on a regenerated 998-billion-row subset of the index, the shards a rebuild from the seed reproduced, give 0.982 and 0.977 for the two kinds at 32 probes and 0.999 and 1.000 at 128. Within the trillion-row index, subsets of servers that differ only in their number of rows show no loss of recall as rows are added, for both query sets, and a gain at narrow widths, the behaviour the Faiss authors describe for a fixed coarse quantizer, here measured at a trillion rows. A prediction from twenty servers, registered before the non-member run was scored, matched its trillion-row recall within error, and a second, from separate corpora of one hundred million to one billion rows, failed at 32 probes. Because a probed cell is scored as the scan scores it, recall at any width is the share of reference neighbours in the probed cells, and that share, computed from cell metadata, matched every measured width, three of them measured after it was recorded. The same identity compares cell orders at equal work, and plain centroid order does as well as the router's radius-adjusted order, so the result is a property of the ordinary inverted file. Holding recall at 32 probes costs a median of 19 billion rows scored per query, 1.9 percent of the index, on all 500 servers. Against float rows regenerated from the seed the compressed ranking keeps at most 0.621 of the true neighbours, so the result is about routing and not about compression. We describe the jobs, the pool and the accounting of the run, and archive the record that the tables and figures are computed from.
