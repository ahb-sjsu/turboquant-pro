What the last group does and does not show. The strata of the arrangement of hyperplanes
l_i = l_j are its faces. Each face is an open piece of its flat, the linear subspace with a
given set of ties, so its tangent space is that flat; that identification is in prose, not
in Lean. Given it, Whitney's condition (a) is the inclusion of flats (whitney_a_flats) and
condition (b) is that limits of rescaled secants lie in the larger flat (whitney_b_secants).
Mathlib has no stratified spaces, so this is not a formalization of Whitney's definition.
